Our programs

Learning, faith, dialogue & service

Programs for children and adults that help our community learn, connect, and give back.

Schedules change throughout the year. Upcoming events are posted on Gathera, or contact us for details.

01

Weekend School

Our weekend school offers children a place to learn, make friends, and grow in a supportive community environment. Contact us for current classes and schedules.

02

Religious Education

We offer religious education for children and adults, taught with care and respect in a welcoming environment. Contact us to learn about current classes.

03

Interfaith Dialogue

We organize interfaith dialogue events that bring together people of different faiths and backgrounds to share perspectives, build mutual understanding, and form lasting friendships.
